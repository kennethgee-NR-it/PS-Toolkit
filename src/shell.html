                    Glossary

                    Appliance mode
                    The video bar runs the meeting software itself (Teams Rooms on Android or Google Meet on Android). No separate computer; the controller pairs over the network.
                    Compute (PC mode)
                    A small dedicated computer runs the meeting software, such as a Lenovo ThinkSmart Core for Teams Rooms on Windows or a Google Meet Compute system. The video bar connects to it by USB.
                    Tap and Tap IP
                    Logitech’s 10.1-inch touch controllers. Tap connects to a compute over a USB or Cat5e kit; Tap IP connects over the network and takes PoE, for appliance-mode rooms.
                    Resource account
                    The room’s own Microsoft 365 account that the Teams Room signs in with, so it can be booked and join meetings. Google Meet rooms are enrolled in the Google Admin console instead.
                    Image height and the 4/6 rule
                    For a 16:9 screen, image height is about 0.49 × the diagonal. A common rule keeps the farthest viewer within 6 × image height for general content, or 4 × for detailed content like spreadsheets.
                    VESA pattern
                    The spacing of the threaded holes on the back of a display, in millimeters, such as 400 × 400. The mount or cart has to match it or use an adapter.
                    Stud, backing, blocking
                    Studs are the vertical framing inside a drywall wall. Backing or blocking is wood or steel added between studs so a mount has something solid to fasten to.
                    SnapToggle (1/4-20)
                    Toggler’s heavy-duty toggle bolt for hollow walls. Drill a 1/2 in hole, fold the steel channel through it, and the channel locks flat behind the drywall. Needs an empty cavity (not a stud), so holes go between studs. Works in drywall and hollow block; plan on one quarter of the ultimate load.
                    Tapcon
                    A blue-coated concrete screw that cuts its own threads. The 1/4 in size uses a 3/16 in carbide bit. Good in concrete, solid brick and filled block; keep it out of mortar joints and away from edges.
                    Wedge and sleeve anchors
                    Expansion anchors for concrete and block. Wedge anchors go into solid concrete; sleeve anchors also suit block. Follow the anchor manufacturer’s hole size, depth and torque.
                    CMU
                    Concrete masonry unit: hollow concrete block. Fasten into the solid face or a grout-filled cell, never into a mortar joint.
                    PoE
                    Power over Ethernet: the network switch powers the device through its network cable, as with Tap IP and Tap Scheduler.
                    HDMI-CEC
                    Lets the room system wake the display and switch inputs over HDMI. Turn it on in the display’s settings so the screen wakes when a meeting starts.
                    Active HDMI, AOC
                    HDMI cables with built-in signal boosting (active) or fiber inside (active optical cable) for runs beyond about 15 ft. They work in one direction only.
                    Bezel
                    The frame around the screen. Heights in this guide are to the outside edge of the bezel (top bezel, bottom bezel), which is what you see and measure on the wall.
                    Wall plate
                    The part of the mount fastened to the wall. The display’s brackets hang on it. Its center height sets where the display ends up.
                    Full-motion mount
                    An articulating arm that lets the display extend, swivel and tilt, such as the Mounting Dream MD2298. It puts more leverage on the wall than a fixed or tilt mount.
                    Calendar resource
                    The room’s own calendar: a Google Workspace resource or a Microsoft 365 room mailbox. People invite it to book the room, and the room system and scheduling panel read it.
                    Zoom Rooms Scheduling Display
                    A panel outside the room, such as a Logitech Tap Scheduler, paired with a Zoom Room to show and book its calendar.
                    Rally Bar 2
                    Logitech’s October 2026 video bar that replaces Rally Bar and Rally Bar Mini, paired with Tap IP 2, Tap Scheduler 2 and Rally Mic Pod 2.
                    RightSight
                    Logitech’s automatic camera framing, which keeps the people in the room centered and sized in the picture.

                    Product details and room sizes come from Logitech’s and Heckler’s published material; weights, VESA patterns and cable limits are typical planning values. Always confirm with the product’s spec sheet and the mount manufacturer’s instructions.
